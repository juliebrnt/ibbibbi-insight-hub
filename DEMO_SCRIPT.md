# Three-minute demo

1. **Open on a phone** (or narrow the browser): Home shows "Good morning", three numbers, the one thing to do next, swipeable insights. Mention that it is a phone-friendly web app that can be installed.
2. **Switch to the laptop.** The banner says "Demo data, not real customers". If it is empty, press **Load demo data** (13 messy exports are read in a few seconds).
3. **Home (Command Centre):** what is happening, why it matters, what to do. The care banner shows we treat distress messages with care, not as marketing. Open **Evidence** on "Reels earn 3x the saves of static posts": every number traces back to the data.
4. **Upload the unknown file:** press **Upload data**, choose `demo_data/mercadillo_ventas.csv` (or "Get a sample unknown file"). It recognises nothing, says so, and keeps it as your own dataset. Press **Turn it into a trend**, name it "Market stalls", **Save**.
5. **See the insight:** back on Home, "Market stalls: caja (€) up 49% in the last three months".
6. **Ask a question:** Ask → "Why did sales dip in January?" or "When should we post?". Open **How I got this**.
7. **Add a decision to Plan:** on Home press **Add to plan** on an insight. The toast shows the starting value it captured. In Plan, the "Ready to review" card computes the change since the decision; type what you learned.
8. **Export the briefing:** More/sidebar → Weekly briefing → Print or save as PDF, or download HTML or Markdown.

Backup lines: Feedback → "Who is missing?" shows the children's line is under-represented in feedback (4% of feedback vs 17% of sales). Social → Opportunity inbox lists partnership enquiries from associations and pharmacies.
